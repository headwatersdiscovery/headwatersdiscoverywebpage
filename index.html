Open science at the source

Headwaters is a not-for-profit lab developing open data, tools, and hypotheses the rest of the field builds on.

  • Parkinson’s Disease: The genes causing Parkinson’s are known, but most variants in them are too rare to classify. We use evolutionary history to separate the damaging changes from the harmless ones — stratifying patients by mechanism and revealing which sites are worth drugging. In progress
  • Protein Engineering: Many promising therapeutic proteins are too unstable or too hard to produce to become medicines. Our open-source software, PhyloBot, reconstructs their evolutionary ancestors — often sturdier, and better starting points for new drugs. Available now →

We’re just getting started. First open releases are planned for 2027.
